on the code.

## You may, without asking

- Say truthfully that your app, wallet, token or tool is "built on VaporChain",
  "for VaporChain" or "compatible with VaporChain".
- Refer to VaporChain by name in articles, tutorials, talks, research, audits
  and comparisons.
- Keep the name in unmodified copies of the software, and in the copyright,
  NOTICE and provenance lines, which the licenses require you to keep.

## You may not, without written permission

- Call a fork, a modified build, a network, a token or a hosted service
  "VaporChain", "Vapor…", or anything confusingly similar.
- Present a fork or another network as the official VaporChain, or suggest that
  the VaporChain project endorses or is affiliated with you.
- Use the VaporChain logos. The only exception is linking to the project.
- Register domains, social handles, token tickers or app names that are likely
  to be mistaken for official VaporChain ones.

## If you fork

Pick a new name and logo, and say it is "based on VaporChain" if you like.
Please also:

- use your own chain IDs, not 7797, 77970 or 779700, so signed transactions
  can't be replayed between networks;
- use your own bech32 prefix and denoms.

Keep the copyright, SPDX, NOTICE and `Provenance:` lines. The AGPL section 7
terms in `NOTICE` require them. Keeping them is attribution; it doesn't mean
endorsement.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue titled "Trademark:" or contact
the maintainer, @muthu2201, on GitHub.
