Open-source EVM appchain for payments

Pay in USDC. One signature. No gas token.

VaporChain is a Cosmos SDK chain with a full EVM, built for consumer payments. Someone holding only USDC can check out with one signature and no seed phrase. Apps keep half of every fee and use it to pay their users' gas.

How a payment works

From one tap to settled USDC

A first-time user with only USDC in their wallet. Five things happen, in this order, inside a single signed operation.

1 · sign

One signature

The wallet signs one ERC-4337 UserOperation. If it is an ordinary address, EIP-7702 upgrades it to a smart account inside the same operation.

2 · sponsor

Policy check

The sponsor service (ERC-7677) confirms the call only touches the app's own contracts and that the app still has gas quota, then signs.

3 · pay gas

Gas covered

A bundler submits it and the paymaster pays gas. Its signature covers the chain ID, the paymaster address and every gas field, so it can't be replayed.

4 · settle

USDC moves

The app's contract calls the Settle precompile at 0x…0900. The chain moves the USDC and splits the fee using integer maths that always adds up exactly.

5 · include

Block rules hold

Validators keep sponsored operations under half of the block, and a fee floor keeps spam out: 0 of 8,962 below-floor transactions got in under load.

Design choices

What is hard to copy

Most chains leave payments, sponsorship and spam limits to apps. VaporChain puts them in the protocol, where every validator enforces them.

Payments are part of consensus

Settlement and revenue sharing run in a chain module, not in app contracts. One module account holds every ledger and invariants are checked every block. If one ever breaks, payments pause and the chain keeps producing blocks.

chain/x/settle

Free for users, bounded for the chain

Apps pay their users' gas, so users see no gas at all. The 50% lane cap is enforced in consensus, which means sponsorship can't be turned into a way to flood the network.

chain/lane

Sponsorship nobody can farm

An app earns gas quota from the fees it pays, or by bonding USDC that it can withdraw after 21 days. Quota grows in a straight line with capital, so splitting one bond across 100 fake apps buys exactly the same gas. No KYC, face scans or gatekeepers.

chain/x/apps

Every transaction pays for compute

Gas is a credit bought only with USDC at a fixed protocol price, and all of it is burned. An unsponsored ERC-20 transfer costs about $0.0017. Paying fees just to farm free gas returns at most $0.90 per dollar.

chain/x/settle · gas burn

Open, with the core protected

The protocol is AGPL-3.0, so a fork has to publish its source even if it only runs the code as a service. The SDK is Apache-2.0, so apps built on it stay fully yours. The name and logo are not licensed.

NOTICE · TRADEMARKS.md

No hidden levers

No admin path can seize funds, freeze a contract or switch a fork off. The guardian can pause for a limited time but can't unpause; only governance can. Removed validators can never rejoin.

Status

Where it stands today

Built and tested

  • Full one-signature USDC checkout, proven end to end on a live local network
  • CI on every change: Go race tests, Solidity tests, SDK and browser tests
  • Red-team pass, a drain audit of every path that moves value, and a written threat model

Not yet

  • Not independently audited
  • No public testnet or mainnet running yet
